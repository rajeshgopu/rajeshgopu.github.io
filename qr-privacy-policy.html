This Privacy Policy explains how [Publisher legal name] ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Quick QR File Share (the "App").

1. Information the App handles

Files and file metadata. When you choose files to send or receive, the App accesses them only to provide the transfer. Files are transferred directly between participating devices using nearby-device connectivity. The App is not designed to upload file contents to our servers or store them in cloud storage.

The App may temporarily process file names, sizes, content URIs, and transfer status on your device. Transfer history is stored locally and contains a local transfer ID, file count, total size, and completion time. It does not contain file contents or QR session secrets.

Camera and QR data. If you receive files, the App uses your camera to scan a QR code. Camera frames and QR session data are used for the connection flow and are not intended to be retained by us.

Device and permission information. The App requests permissions needed for camera scanning, nearby-device connections, notifications, and compatible Android networking features. Permission status and basic app settings may be stored locally.

2. Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage and improve reliability. Events may include actions such as opening a screen, scanning a QR code, starting or cancelling a transfer, and a transfer state. We configure analytics events not to include file contents, file names, QR secrets, private storage paths, or permanent device identifiers.

Firebase may process information according to Google's policies and the configuration of the Firebase project. See Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising

The App may display advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may collect or use information such as advertising identifiers, approximate location, device information, and ad interaction data, subject to your Android settings, consent choices, and applicable law. We do not send file contents, file names, QR secrets, or private storage paths to advertisers.

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. How information is used

Information handled by the App is used to provide direct file transfers, scan QR codes, show transfer progress and notifications, maintain local transfer history, measure app usage, display advertisements, prevent abuse, and maintain and improve the App.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your files. Files are shared with another device only when you initiate or accept a transfer. We may receive services from providers such as Google Firebase and Google Mobile Ads, which process information as described above. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and the App.

6. Retention and deletion

Files and local history remain under your control on your device. You can delete received files and clear local App data using Android settings or the App's available controls. Analytics and advertising providers retain information according to their policies and controls. We do not operate a server-side file repository for transfers.

7. Your choices

  • Grant or revoke Android permissions in Android Settings.
  • Control advertising personalization and reset your advertising ID through Android settings.
  • Limit or disable analytics where supported by your device or Google account settings.
  • Contact us using the details below to ask questions about personal information handled by the App.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can assess and address the issue.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the App's design. No method of electronic transfer or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for choosing what to share and for protecting access to your device.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, its providers, or applicable requirements change. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date.
