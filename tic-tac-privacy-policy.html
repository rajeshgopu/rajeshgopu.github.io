Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

1. Introduction

Tic Tac Toe: Online XO Game ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide

  • Player Name: Optional display name you choose for your profile
  • Game Statistics: Wins, losses, draws, streaks, and game history (stored locally on your device)

2.2 Automatically Collected Data

  • Anonymous User ID: Firebase Authentication generates an anonymous ID to enable multiplayer features
  • Game Data: Room IDs, board states, moves, and chat messages for online multiplayer games
  • Device Information: Basic device info for analytics (Firebase Analytics)
  • Advertising ID: For personalized ads (Google AdMob)

2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personally identifiable information (name, email, phone, location)
  • No contacts, photos, or files from your device
  • No microphone or camera access

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Multiplayer Games: Match players, synchronize game state, enable chat
  • Statistics: Track your wins, losses, streaks locally
  • Analytics: Improve app performance and user experience (Firebase Analytics)
  • Advertising: Show relevant ads (Google AdMob)
  • Fraud Prevention: Detect abuse in multiplayer matches

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Local Storage: Player name, statistics, preferences stored on your device using SharedPreferences
  • Cloud Storage (Firebase): Online game rooms, moves, chat messages stored in Firestore
  • Data Retention: Game rooms auto-expire after 30 minutes; completed rooms cleaned up automatically
  • Encryption: All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS

4. Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Data Shared
Firebase Authentication Anonymous user accounts for multiplayer Anonymous UID only
Cloud Firestore Real-time game state synchronization Room data, moves, chat messages
Firebase Analytics App usage analytics Anonymous events, device info
Google AdMob Advertising Advertising ID, device info
Google Play Games Services Leaderboards, achievements, saved games Player profile (name, avatar), game progress, achievements
Important: We do not sell your personal data. Third-party services are used solely to provide app functionality. Their privacy policies apply to data they process.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access/Delete: You can reset all local data by uninstalling the app
  • Online Data: Game rooms auto-expire; you can request manual deletion by contacting us
  • Analytics Opt-out: Disable "Analytics" in device settings or use Firebase opt-out
  • Ads Personalization: Opt out via Google Ads Settings or device settings

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed on Firebase servers located in the United States and other countries where Google operates. By using the app, you consent to this transfer.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated date. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, we process data based on:

  • Legitimate Interest: Analytics, fraud prevention, app improvement
  • Contract Performance: Multiplayer game synchronization
  • Consent: Personalized advertising (where required)
